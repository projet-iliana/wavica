WAVICA complements your wearable

Oura, Apple Watch and other devices are excellent at sensing and reporting. WAVICA is designed as the adaptive action layer: it detects meaningful personal change, adds context, proposes one practical experiment, checks the outcome and learns from your feedback.

This demo uses synthetic example data. It is a product prototype, not a medical device.
